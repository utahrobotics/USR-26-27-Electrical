- 3-slots + 1 edge connector design
- Integrated power-delivery board circuitry
- Each slot has its on fuse on the 24v line
- Each slot can theoretically deliver up to 4A on the 24v rail; that is a whole 96 watts of power *per slot*. I certainly hope that we do not need that much power.
- Three Anderson connectors are present for auxiliary 24v power routing.
- XT-90 connector for 24v power-in to the board
- Integrated down-regulation of 24v to 5v for logic-level power
